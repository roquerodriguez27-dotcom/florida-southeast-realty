# Seller weekly-report fulfillment

## What the website request does

The worksheet at `/planning/sell?tool=report` creates a manual draft from the
information entered on the page. A submitted draft is tagged
`planning-seller-report` in the inquiry context; its numbers are user-entered,
not an automatic feed of verified listing activity.

A saved request gives Roque the seller's submitted context and preferred reply
details. It is not itself a completed weekly report, a verified listing agreement,
or enrollment in an automatic email series. Treat the first request as a request
to discuss the reporting arrangement. Confirm the property, representation,
recipients, delivery method, cadence and start date before promising delivery.

Use the actual saved request and its form/context in the private CRM. Do not
manufacture a request, mark an undelivered report as sent, or send a test to a
real client's contact details.

## Set up the real arrangement

Record the responsible broker, property, seller-authorized recipients, agreed
weekly day/time, delivery method, reporting window, start/end conditions and
where the underlying records are kept. Confirm what the seller wants to decide
from the report. A request for a report does not authorize an ad campaign, new
spend, a price change, or contact with third parties.

## Prepare each reporting period

1. Establish a start and end date and timezone. Gather current records for that
   period from the authorized listing, showing, marketing and CRM systems.
2. Separate scheduled showings from completed showings, inquiries from website
   views, and unique prospects from repeated contacts. API requests are not
   visitors or inquiries. Record the source and definitions of any count.
3. Summarize documented feedback without identifying buyers or exposing private
   contact or financial information. Label a missing response as no feedback
   received; do not replace it with a guessed sentiment.
4. Note relevant new, changed, pending or closed competing listings only when
   the data is current and permitted to be used. Keep a dated source record.
   Do not invent comparable sales or imply a guaranteed valuation.
5. List work actually completed and work planned for the next period. Separate
   completed marketing from a draft, scheduled post or proposed ad.
6. State the decision that needs the seller's input, with the broker's reasoning
   and relevant uncertainty. Price or spending changes require the appropriate
   separate authorization; they are not executed by generating this report.

## Review, delivery and follow-through

Roque reviews the figures, wording, property, date range and intended recipients.
Use the agreed channel after the reporting arrangement is confirmed. Record
actual delivery time and outcome in the brokerage's system; a draft, attempted
send or notification that failed is not successful delivery. Correct material
errors promptly and retain the corrected version and reason.

Follow up on requested decisions using the seller's agreed communication method.
If a source is unavailable, report that limitation and the last verified period;
do not silently reuse last week's numbers under this week's dates.

Automating report delivery later requires a separate implementation with verified
data retrieval, recipient authorization, scheduling, delivery tracking and an
end/pause mechanism. The current planning page does not establish those services
by itself.
