# Roque's six short video scripts

**Status: scripts only. These videos have not been recorded or published.**
Roque must record the spoken material himself, review the claims on the recording
date, and approve the finished clip. The website currently offers written FAQs
and a question form; it does not present these scripts as recorded videos.

Each script is intended for roughly 35–55 seconds at a natural pace. Use the
recording guide in `docs/broker-video-recording-guide.md`. Keep qualifications in
the spoken clip and captions, not only in a distant description.

## 1. What the 0.5% listing-side fee means

**Spoken draft**

“I'm Roque Rodriguez with Florida Southeast Realty. Our advertised listing-side
brokerage fee is half of one percent. That is one item in your selling costs.
Commission rates are negotiable, and any authorized buyer-broker compensation
and other closing costs are separate. Before you make a decision, ask me for a
written proposal showing the services, terms and estimated costs for your home.
Then compare the likely net proceeds using the same assumptions. You can start
with our seller planning worksheet and bring me the questions behind the numbers.”

**On-screen title:** Understand the listing-side fee.

**Destination:** `/planning/sell` and `/sellers`.

**Before recording:** Confirm the current advertised offer and proposed services
against the brokerage's written listing terms. Do not claim a guaranteed net,
sale price, sale time, or savings against an invented competing commission.

## 2. The monthly cost beyond the mortgage

**Spoken draft**

“When you're comparing two homes, compare the monthly ownership costs too. Start
with the loan payment, then add property taxes, insurance, association charges
and a maintenance allowance. An estimate is a starting point. Ask the lender,
insurance agent and association for information specific to the property, and
look for assessments or other costs that may not appear in a listing. Our buyer
planning tools help you put those questions in one place. Save your worksheet,
then let's review what is known and what still needs a quote or a document.”

**On-screen title:** Compare the whole ownership cost.

**Destination:** `/planning/buy` and `/buyer-tools`.

**Before recording:** Show blank or clearly labeled illustrative inputs. Do not
display a fictional approval, insurance quote, live MLS price or client budget.

## 4. Put the 2027 homestead date on your calendar

**Spoken draft**

“If you need to apply for a Palm Beach County homestead exemption for 2027, put
March first on your calendar. The Property Appraiser lists March first, or the
next business day when it falls on a weekend, as the annual filing deadline.
March first, 2027 is a Monday. Confirm the requirements and your eligibility
with that office. Our owner planner can export this date alongside insurance
and maintenance dates you enter. Import the file and choose reminders in your
calendar app. A reminder does not file the exemption application for you.”

**On-screen title:** 2027 homestead filing · March 1.

**Destination:** `/planning/own#calendar`.

**Official source:** [PBC Property Appraiser important dates](https://pbcpao.gov/important-dates.htm).

**Before recording:** Recheck the official date and rule. This is a dated 2027
script. Retire or clearly archive it after the filing period; do not silently
reuse it for another year or turn it into individualized eligibility advice.

## 5. A free notice when a property document is recorded

**Spoken draft**

“Palm Beach County owners can register for the Clerk's free Property Fraud Alert
service. It sends an email notice when a recorded document matches the name,
address or parcel number you registered. Follow the Clerk's instructions for
matching variations and complete the confirmation email yourself. These alerts
tell you about recording activity. They do not prevent a document from being
recorded or guarantee protection against fraud. Review any unfamiliar activity
and follow the Clerk's guidance. The official registration link is on our owner
planning page, so you can go directly to the county service.”

**On-screen title:** Property-fraud alerts · Register with the Clerk.

**Destination:** `/planning/own#property-alerts`.

**Official source:** [Palm Beach County Clerk Property Fraud Alert](https://www.mypalmbeachclerk.com/services/property-fraud-alert).

**Before recording:** Recheck the service description. Do not promise prevention,
title insurance, a fixed response time, or that FSR has enrolled the viewer.

## 6. A condo fee is the beginning of the review

**Spoken draft**

“A condo's monthly fee is only part of the ownership picture. Ask for the
association's current budget, reserve information, applicable inspection and
reserve-study reports, and assessment notices. Look at how planned work will
be funded and when payments may be due. The answers depend on the building,
the documents and the rules that apply to it. Our homeowner update links to
official guidance and gives you a document checklist. Bring your questions
to the association and the qualified professionals advising your purchase,
and let me help you organize the property review.”

**On-screen title:** Ask for the documents behind the fee.

**Destination:** `/research/homeowner-updates#condo-reserves-and-assessments`.

**Official sources:** [Florida statute 718.112](https://flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/0718.112)
and [DBPR condominium FAQs](https://condos.myfloridalicense.com/faqs/).

**Before recording:** Follow the source-review policy in the homeowner updates
runbook. Do not say every building has the same study, deadline or assessment.
