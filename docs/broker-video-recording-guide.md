# Recording and publishing Roque's short videos

## What is ready, and what is still needed

Six spoken drafts are in `docs/broker-video-scripts.md`. Roque's real recordings,
approved captions/transcripts, and finished video files are still needed. No
synthetic broker voice, fake client interview, stock footage labeled as a real
transaction, or unrecorded video should appear as completed work.

The public `/ask-roque` page works with written FAQs and its question form today.
Keep that useful experience while the videos are being recorded.

## One simple recording session

1. Review each script's factual qualifications and official links. Confirm the
   brokerage's current fee and service terms. Mark the recording date and the
   date each time-sensitive source was checked.
2. Use a quiet, well-lit space and a steady phone at eye level. Record vertically
   for a short social clip, keeping the face and any captions clear of the edges.
   Make a brief sound check before recording the whole script.
3. Record two natural takes of each script. Pause briefly before and after the
   spoken material. Keep the qualification and the final next step in the take.
4. Use Roque, the actual brokerage setting, and approved property footage. Obtain
   permission before showing an owner, occupied interior, address or document.
   Keep client contact details, signatures, financial records and access codes
   out of frame.
5. Review the complete edit. Correct captions against the audio, including
   percentages, dates and names. Add a short readable title, brokerage identity,
   and one relevant page URL. Keep a plain-text transcript with the final file.
6. Have Roque approve the exact video, caption, destination and any dated claim
   before publication. Recording approval alone does not authorize paid ad spend.

## Handoff for the website

Provide the actual final MP4 or hosted-video URL, a real poster frame, the approved
transcript/captions, actual duration, recording date, topic and destination.
Use descriptive filenames such as `roque-homeowner-calendar-2027.mp4`; do not
create empty files as placeholders. Add the player only after the real asset
works, with controls, a transcript/captions and a meaningful title. Avoid autoplay
with sound. Verify phone-sized playback and the destination link.

For a public case study, follow `docs/broker-case-study-intake.md` first. A generic
educational video must not be dressed up as a verified client success story.

## Maintenance

Recheck dates and legal/insurance qualifications before posting a clip or reusing
it in a campaign. The 2027 homestead clip needs a visible year and a review after
its filing period. The homeowner calendar exports a static file; it is not an
email subscription and it does not update an imported calendar automatically.

If a fact changes, correct the transcript/caption and replace or archive the old
video as appropriate. Record what changed. Do not update a displayed recording
date or imply a new recording when only the description changed.
