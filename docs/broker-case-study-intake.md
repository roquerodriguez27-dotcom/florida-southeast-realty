# Real case-study intake and publication template

**This is a blank intake and editorial template. No completed client case study
is supplied or implied.** Keep evidence and private intake details in the
brokerage's authorized records, not in this public repository. Only an approved,
redacted public version belongs on the website.

Use the brokerage's actual permission process. This worksheet is not a signed
release or a substitute for one. A permission to discuss a transaction privately
does not by itself establish permission to publish a testimonial or property image.

## Evidence review

- Confirm every number against its underlying record. Identify whether a figure
  is a list price, contract price, recorded closing amount, estimate or net amount.
- Define the comparison and assumptions before claiming savings. Do not invent
  a competing commission, quote or counterfactual sale price.
- Separate the brokerage's work from the work of the lender, inspector, attorney,
  title company, insurance agent and association. Do not claim sole credit for
  an outcome that cannot be attributed that way.
- Report the actual period and outcome. A property going under contract is not
  a completed closing. A projection is not an achieved result.
- Obtain approval for the specific public details. Remove unnecessary addresses,
  account details, signatures, personal circumstances and identifiable buyer
  information. An anonymized case still needs a real evidence record.
- Keep material limitations: a result for one transaction does not promise the
  same price, savings, timing, financing or insurance for another person.

## Publication gate

Roque reviews the full draft against the evidence and client permissions, then
approves the exact public version. Keep the approval and source record privately.
Check the page, images, captions and structured data for consistency before it
goes live. Do not add testimonial stars, a video, numerical results or a client
name merely to make the template look complete.
